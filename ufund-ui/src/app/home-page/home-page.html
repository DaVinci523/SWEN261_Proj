Vision Statement

Student Research is one of the most important yet underappreciated drivers of advancement in society. From the World Wide Web to the the Covid 19-mRNA vaccine, countless breakthroughs have have come from student researchers. At MyResearch, we are seeking to help fund these innovators as they shape the future. Please consider joining us in our goal so you can be a part of building a better tommorow.
